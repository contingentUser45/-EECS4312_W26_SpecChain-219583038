# Application: Calm - Pipeline Reflection

-   **Biggest and most crucial difference between pipelines:**  
- With the automatic pipeline, we saw the least amount of ambiguity and the highest traceability when using exactly the same 1678 reviews dataset. Auto captured high level user types with the strongest grounding in specific review segments. In contrast, the manual pipeline produced more focused and nuanced personas that were directly tied to particular review groups, resulting in better relevance to the actual context of the reviews. The hybrid pipeline combined both by refining the automated outputs to improve clarity and correctness.
-   **Most useful pipeline:**  
- The Automatic pipeline was the most useful overall as it effectively worked with specially created prompts. This resulted in outputs that were both structured and meaningfully linked to personas, making them more practical for downstream tasks. However, the outputs were sometimes more general compared to the manual pipeline, which captured more specific user context.
-   **Most surprising finding:**  
- With the high testability rating and strong review coverage compared to the other pipelines, the automated pipeline shows that with the right prompt creations and commands, it can gain very strong results and produce consistent outputs.
-   **Observed weakness in the automated pipeline:**  
- Even though it performs well in most of the fields relevant to the system, the automated pipeline may not fully capture deeper context as effectively as manual input. In some cases, this leads to more general outputs that needed refinement in the hybrid pipeline.
